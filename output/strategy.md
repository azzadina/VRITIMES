# VRITIMES social strategy — batch 16 Nov – 11 Dec 2026

## Positioning
VRITIMES is the practical, affordable way for start-ups and SMEs in Southeast Asia and Australia to turn real business news into published coverage: write → distribute → report in one dashboard. Social content earns that authority by teaching what PR managers actually need (journalist data, Google guidance, templates) in a confident, slightly playful voice, and only then asking for the click.

## Pillar logic (per country, 12 posts)

## Schedule rationale
Mon = EDU carousel (LinkedIn PDF performs best early week, 08:00–09:00 local). Wed = FUN/VIRAL for evening IG/FB reach (19:30–20:30). Fri rotates VIRAL → PROMO → EDU → PROMO. W2 Fri promo lands on Black Friday (27 Nov) with a "credibility isn't on sale" hook; W4 Fri promo lands just before 12.12 / local holiday slowdowns. PH 30 Nov (Bonifacio Day) and TH 7 Dec (substitution holiday) posts keep evening IG/FB slots; consider shifting LinkedIn to 10:00 on those days.
