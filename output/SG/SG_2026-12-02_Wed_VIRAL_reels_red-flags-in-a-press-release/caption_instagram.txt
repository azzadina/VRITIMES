Red flags in a press release 🚩 (editors notice, trust us)

🚩 A headline with no verb
🚩 The news buried in paragraph four
🚩 "World's first" with no proof
🚩 A 600-word pitch (69% of journalists prefer under 200 words)
🚩 Media contact: info@

✅ Green flag: news first, proof attached, a real person to call.
