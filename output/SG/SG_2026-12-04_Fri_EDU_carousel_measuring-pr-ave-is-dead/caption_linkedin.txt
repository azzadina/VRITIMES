If your PR report leads with advertising value equivalency, it is measuring the wrong thing.
The industry framework has said so for years, and more bluntly in 2025.

AMEC's Barcelona Principles 3.0 (2020) state that "AVEs are not the value of communication". The 2025 update, Barcelona Principles 4.0, goes further: invalid measures such as AVEs should not be used.
A one-page report we recommend for SME and start-up teams:

1. The objective, agreed before the release went out.
2. Coverage by quality: target audience and tier, not just article count.
3. Key message pick-up: did the point you wanted actually appear?
4. Outcomes: referral visits, sign-ups, enquiries.
5. One thing you will change next time.
