SMEs make up 99% of Singapore's businesses and employ 71% of its workforce.
They account for a much smaller share of business coverage.

The figure, cited by MTI in November 2024, usually frames policy discussions. We read it as a communications gap: tens of thousands of firms whose milestones never reach a journalist.
Not because they lack news value, but because they are not packaged as news. A first overseas office, a new product or a sizeable hiring round is a strong story when it comes with clear numbers and a quotable spokesperson.

If you run an SME: which milestone from this year should the market know about?
