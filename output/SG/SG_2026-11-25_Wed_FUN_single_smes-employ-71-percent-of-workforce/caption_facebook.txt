Fun fact: SMEs employ 71% of Singapore's workforce. If you run one, your news deserves coverage too 💙

From MTI (Minister Grace Fu, Nov 2024):
• SMEs = 99% of Singapore's businesses
• They employ 71% of the total workforce

A regional expansion, a new grant-funded product, 30 new hires: all newsworthy.
They just rarely reach a newsroom in a usable format.
