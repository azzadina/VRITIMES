The brands that appear in the news around big moments prepared their releases weeks earlier.
Here is a 2027 PR calendar framework for Singapore.

With SMEs making up 99% of Singapore's businesses (MTI, 2024), news days around Budget, National Day and year-end are crowded. Preparation, not volume, decides who gets covered.
A quarterly framework to adapt:

1. Q1: Chinese New Year (publish before the slowdown), Budget 2027 commentary and annual results.
2. Q2: mid-year customer data, hiring and regional expansion, and pre-announcements for trade shows.
3. Q3: National Day community stories, plus partnership and funding news.
4. Q4: 11.11, Black Friday and 12.12, then plan January announcements before the mid-December slowdown.
5. For every moment: T-14 research, T-7 final release, T-3 pitch, Day 0 publish, T+3 recap.
