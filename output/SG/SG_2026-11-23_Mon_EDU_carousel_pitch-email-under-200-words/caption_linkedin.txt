The pitches journalists reply to are usually shorter than you think.
According to Muck Rack, 69% of journalists prefer pitches under 200 words.

The most common mistake we see: pasting the whole release into the pitch. A pitch is an invitation to read, not the release itself.
The same Muck Rack State of Journalism 2025 data settles the follow-up question too: 50% of journalists say one follow-up is ideal, and 51% want it 3–7 days later.
A four-line structure your team can use straight away:

1. Relevance: why this topic fits their beat or readers.
2. The news in one sentence, with a number.
3. The offer: data, a spokesperson or photos that are ready now.
4. A link to the full release and a contact who responds.
