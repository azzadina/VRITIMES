Trust in news in Singapore is 45%, the second highest of the six Asia-Pacific markets we work in, according to the Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2025.
That makes earned coverage especially valuable.

For comparison: Thailand 55%, Australia 43%, the Philippines 38%, Malaysia 37% and Indonesia 36%.
When audiences trust established media, being covered by it lends credibility that paid placements rarely match. Practical implications:

1. Invest in news value: data, clear impact, a named spokesperson.
2. Target the outlets your customers actually read.
3. Reuse coverage in sales decks, newsletters and social, with links to the source.
