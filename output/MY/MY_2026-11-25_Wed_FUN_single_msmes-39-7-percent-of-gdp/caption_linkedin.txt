Malaysian MSMEs contributed 39.7% of GDP in 2025 and account for 96.1% of business establishments.
Their share of business headlines is far smaller.

DOSM's latest MSME release usually gets read as an economic indicator. We read it as a communications gap: hundreds of thousands of businesses with stories that never reach a journalist.
Not because they aren't newsworthy, but because they aren't packaged as news. A third outlet, a first export contract or 50 new hires is a strong local story when it comes with clear numbers and a quotable spokesperson.

If you run an SME: which story from this year should the public know about?
