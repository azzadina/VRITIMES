Fun fact: MSMEs made up 39.7% of Malaysia's GDP in 2025. If your business is one of them, your story deserves coverage too 💙

From DOSM's MSMEs Performance 2025:
• MSME GDP grew 5.7%
• MSMEs = 39.7% of national GDP
• And 96.1% of all establishments (OECD, citing DOSM 2024)

New outlets, first exports, 50 new hires: these are local news stories.
They just rarely get written in a format newsrooms can use.
