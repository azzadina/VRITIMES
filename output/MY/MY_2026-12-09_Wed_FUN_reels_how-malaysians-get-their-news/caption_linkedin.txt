Reuters Institute's 2025 data shows 40% of Malaysians use TikTok for news, while only 37% trust most news most of the time.
What does that mean for brand communications?

The two numbers look contradictory but work together. Audiences discover stories in the feed, then judge credibility by the source.
For brands, the implications are practical:

1. Coverage in established media gives people a source they can check.
2. That coverage should be reshared on your social channels, including short video.
3. The message must match across media and social, or doubt creeps in.
