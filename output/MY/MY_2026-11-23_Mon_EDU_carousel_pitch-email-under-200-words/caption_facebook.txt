Here's a pitch email template under 200 words. Four lines, ready for your team to use 👇

The 4-line structure:
1️⃣ Why it matters to their beat
2️⃣ The news in one sentence + a number
3️⃣ What you can offer: data, spokesperson, photos
4️⃣ Release link + contact

Follow-up? Once, 3–7 days later.
