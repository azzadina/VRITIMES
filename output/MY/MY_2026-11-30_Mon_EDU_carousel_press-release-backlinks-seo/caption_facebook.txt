Lots of teams send press releases just for backlinks. Before you do, here is what Google actually says 👇

Google lists optimised anchor text in press releases distributed on other sites as link spam.

The safe way:
✓ Anchor = brand name / URL
✓ 1–2 links to a relevant page
✓ News that earns follow-up coverage

The real value: referral traffic, brand mentions and organic coverage.
