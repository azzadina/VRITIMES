The first 24 hours of a crisis decide the headline. Here's the playbook. ⏱️

0–1h: confirm facts, name one spokesperson
1–3h: holding statement (know / doing / next update)
3–6h: BM + English, identical facts
6–24h: update exactly when you promised

Never: "no comment", deleting comments, public blame, guessed numbers.

Only 37% of Malaysians trust most news most of the time (Reuters Institute, 2025). Rumours fill silence.
