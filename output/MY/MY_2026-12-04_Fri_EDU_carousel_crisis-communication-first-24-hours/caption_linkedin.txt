In a crisis, the story is written in the first 24 hours, with or without you.
A practical playbook for Malaysian SMEs.

Reuters Institute's Digital News Report 2025 puts trust in news in Malaysia at 37%. In a low-trust environment rumours spread faster, and a clear, sourced statement becomes the thing journalists can rely on.
The timeline we recommend:

1. 0–1 hour: confirm facts with the people closest to the issue. One spokesperson, one approver.
2. 1–3 hours: a holding statement covering what you know, what you're doing and when the next update will come.
3. 3–6 hours: publish in Bahasa Malaysia and English simultaneously with identical facts.
4. 6–24 hours: update on the schedule you promised, even if the update is "investigation ongoing".
5. Avoid: "no comment", deleting criticism, blaming individuals publicly, or guessing numbers.
