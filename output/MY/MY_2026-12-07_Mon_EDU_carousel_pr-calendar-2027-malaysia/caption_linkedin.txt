The brands that appear in the news around big moments usually prepared their releases weeks before.
Here is a 2027 PR calendar framework for Malaysia.

Recurring data releases are underrated news hooks. DOSM's MSME report, which put MSMEs at 39.7% of GDP in 2025, lands every year. A reaction release with your own numbers on publication day is an easy win.
A quarterly framework to adapt:

1. Q1: Chinese New Year, Ramadan and Hari Raya (2027 dates are estimates), plus your annual milestones.
2. Q2: mid-year data, hiring and expansion, and product launches ahead of mid-year sales.
3. Q3: DOSM's MSME release in late July, then Merdeka and Malaysia Day with community programmes.
4. Q4: Budget 2028 commentary, Deepavali, 11.11 and 12.12. Send promo releases before peak days.
5. For every moment: T-14 research, T-7 final release, T-3 pitch, Day 0 publish, T+3 recap.
