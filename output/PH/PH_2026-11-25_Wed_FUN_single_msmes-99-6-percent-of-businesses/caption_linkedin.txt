MSMEs make up 99.6% of Philippine businesses and generate 66.97% of jobs.
Their share of business headlines is a fraction of that.

DTI's 2023 figures (1,246,373 enterprises, 6,351,466 MSME jobs) are usually quoted in policy discussions. We read them as a communications gap: more than a million businesses with stories that never reach a reporter.
Not because they lack news value, but because they aren't packaged as news. A third branch in Davao, a first export order or 50 new hires is a strong local story when it comes with clear numbers and a quotable spokesperson.

If you run an MSME: which story from this year should the public hear?
