Fun fact: 99.6% of businesses in the Philippines are MSMEs. If yours is one of them, your story deserves coverage too 💙

DTI 2023 MSME Statistics:
• 1,246,373 business enterprises
• 99.6% are MSMEs
• MSMEs generated 6,351,466 jobs = 66.97% of employment

New branches, first exports, 50 new hires: these are real news stories.
They just rarely get written in a format newsrooms can use.
