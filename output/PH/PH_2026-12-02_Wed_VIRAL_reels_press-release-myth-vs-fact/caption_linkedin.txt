Four press release myths we still hear every week, and what the data actually says.

Most PR habits are inherited, not tested. Here are the four we see most often, checked against current sources:

1. "Press releases are dead." Cision's 2025 State of the Media found 72% of journalists call them the most useful resource PR teams provide.
2. "A longer pitch looks more serious." Muck Rack (2025): 69% of journalists prefer pitches under 200 words.
3. "Follow up daily to be sure." Same study: 50% say one follow-up is ideal, and 51% want it 3–7 days later.
4. "Releases are an SEO shortcut." Google's spam policies list optimized anchor text in distributed press releases as link spam.
