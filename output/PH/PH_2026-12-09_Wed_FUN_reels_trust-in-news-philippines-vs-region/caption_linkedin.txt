Trust in news in the Philippines sits at 38%, according to the Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2025.
That changes how brands should communicate.

For context, the same survey puts Thailand at 55%, Singapore at 45%, Australia at 43%, Malaysia at 37% and Indonesia at 36%.
In a lower-trust environment, claims are discounted and proof is rewarded. For brand announcements, that means:

1. Lead with verifiable data, not adjectives.
2. Name a real spokesperson who can be contacted.
3. Cite your sources and link to them.
4. Get published by established media, then share that coverage on social.
