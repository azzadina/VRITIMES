A fun fact for marketing and PR teams: the first press release appeared in 1906, after a train crash. The story is in this carousel 👇

28 October 1906, Atlantic City (USA).
Railways usually stayed silent and kept reporters out.

Ivy Lee wrote an official statement and handed it to the press instead.
The New York Times printed it word for word on 30 October 1906.

Still true today: speak first, use facts, make reporters' jobs easier.
