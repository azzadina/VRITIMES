The quote is the only part of a press release where a person speaks. Most companies waste it.
How to write one a journalist will actually use.

Cision's 2025 State of the Media found 72% of journalists call press releases the most useful resource from PR teams. What makes a release usable is that parts of it can be lifted as is, and the quote is the most common candidate.
Four rules we apply:

1. The "thrilled" test: if the quote fits any company's release, rewrite it.
2. Opinion over summary: explain why it matters or what happens next.
3. One number, phrased the way a person would say it.
4. Under 40 words, two sentences: the point, then the reason.
