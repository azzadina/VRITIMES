The brands that show up in the news around big moments prepared their releases weeks before.
Here is a 2027 PR calendar framework for the Philippines.

With 1,246,373 enterprises in the country (DTI, 2023), competition for attention around peak moments is real. Preparation, not volume, decides who gets covered.
A quarterly framework to adapt:

1. Q1: annual milestones, Holy Week (newsrooms slow, so publish before), summer and graduation angles.
2. Q2: Independence Day on 12 June, back-to-school and mid-year customer data.
3. Q3: MSME Development Month in July, National Heroes Day, and the start of the "ber" months for Christmas planning.
4. Q4: 11.11 and 12.12, Bonifacio Day and the Christmas slowdown. Publish early.
5. For every moment: T-14 research, T-7 final release, T-3 pitch, Day 0 publish, T+3 recap.
