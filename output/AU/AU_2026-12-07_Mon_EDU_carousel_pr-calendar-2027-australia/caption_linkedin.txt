A 2027 PR calendar framework for Australia.

Small businesses make up 97% of actively trading businesses in Australia (ASBFEO, 2026), and they all compete for the same news days. Preparation, not volume, decides who gets covered.
A quarterly framework to adapt:

1. Q1: the mid-January return to work, when newsrooms are hungry for news, plus half-year results.
2. Q2: Federal Budget commentary (usually May) and EOFY angles.
3. Q3: new financial year plans, hires and pricing, plus partnership and funding news.
4. Q4: Black Friday and Cyber Monday, then publish before the Christmas shutdown.
5. For every moment: T-14 research, T-7 final release, T-3 pitch, Day 0 publish, T+3 recap.
