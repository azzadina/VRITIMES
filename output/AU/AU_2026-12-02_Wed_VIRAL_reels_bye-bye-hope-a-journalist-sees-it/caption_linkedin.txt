Most small-business media strategies quietly rely on hope.
Four hopes worth replacing with a process.

Muck Rack's State of Journalism 2025 gives a useful baseline: 69% of journalists prefer pitches under 200 words, 50% say one follow-up is ideal, and 51% want it 3–7 days after the pitch.

1. Instead of hoping a journalist finds your social post, send the news to the right person.
2. Instead of hoping they open the attachment, put the release text in the email body.
3. Instead of hoping length signals seriousness, keep the pitch under 200 words.
4. Instead of chasing daily, follow up once, 3–7 days later.

Which "hope" is still in your team's media plan?
