Say bye bye to "hope a journalist sees it" 👋

"Hope they find our LinkedIn post" → send the news directly
"Hope they open the attachment" → text in the email body
"Hope a long pitch looks serious" → 69% prefer under 200 words
"Hope they reply if I chase daily" → one follow-up, 3–7 days later

Hope isn't a media strategy. A process is.
