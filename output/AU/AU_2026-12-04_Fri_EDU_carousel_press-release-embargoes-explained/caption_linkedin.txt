An embargo is one of the few tools that gives journalists more time to do a better story.
Used badly, it is also the fastest way to lose their trust.

Newsrooms are stretched. Muck Rack's State of Journalism 2025 found 62% of journalists took on more responsibilities this year. An embargo helps when it gives them time to prepare; it hurts when it adds admin or confusion.
How we recommend using one:

1. Reserve it for news that needs preparation: research, funding, launches with demos.
2. Ask first. Share details only after the journalist agrees to the embargo.
3. State the date, time and timezone in full, e.g. 6:00am AEDT, Tuesday 24 November 2026.
4. Give reasonable lead time and send every asset in one package.
5. Have a plan if it breaks early: publish your own version immediately.
