Fun fact: Australia has more than 2.7 million small businesses. If yours is one of them, your news deserves a run too.

From the Australian Small Business and Family Enterprise Ombudsman:
• 2,741,087 actively trading small businesses (30 June 2026)
• Up 3.2% on a year earlier
• 97% of all actively trading businesses

A new site, a first export deal, 20 new hires: genuinely newsworthy.
