There were 2,741,087 actively trading small businesses in Australia at 30 June 2026, 97% of all businesses.
Their share of business coverage is a fraction of that.

ASBFEO's figures are mostly used in policy debates. We see a communications gap: millions of businesses with milestones that never reach a journalist.
Not because they lack news value, but because they aren't packaged as news. A second location, a first export contract or a hiring round is a solid local story when it comes with clear numbers and a spokesperson who will take a call.

If you run a small business: what from this year deserved a story?
