A small habit that stops good releases being read: sending them as PDF attachments.
It sounds trivial. The impact isn't.

Muck Rack's State of Journalism 2025 found 69% of journalists prefer pitches under 200 words. Journalists want to decide quickly, without extra steps.
A PDF attachment adds three of them: download, open, copy. Pair it with a generic subject line and the odds of being read drop sharply.

1. Paste the release text straight into the email body.
2. Use the release headline as the subject line.
3. Share photos and supporting documents by link.
4. If you use a distribution platform, make sure the release lives on a web page that can be accessed and quoted.
