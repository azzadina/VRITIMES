News avoidance in Australia is 69%, according to the Digital News Report: Australia 2025.
If your audience is skimming, your announcement needs to work harder.

The same research found social media has overtaken going directly to news websites, while the Reuters Institute puts trust in news at 43%, up three points.
For business announcements, that suggests a few practical rules:

1. Lead with one clear point in plain language.
2. Use real numbers instead of adjectives.
3. Make it easy to share: a clean web page, a strong image, a short summary.
4. Reuse earned coverage on your own social channels, where people now find their news.
